Research at Johns Hopkins

HopDoc: Clinical Reasoning System on REACH EHR Data CDHAI · In Progress

Clinical foundation model initiative on the Johns Hopkins REACH de-identified EHR dataset for congestive heart failure readmission and mortality prediction.

  • Profiled 8.3M patients (3.2M with clinical notes, 1.12B structured OMOP events) and designed the CHF cohort: 103,577 patients, 17.1% 30-day readmission, 28.8% mortality
  • Developed a staged 4-phase IRB access strategy (single-condition retrospective through generalist CDS), escalated to school leadership
  • Starting with fine-tuned clinical BERT encoder for binary and time-to-event readmission classification, with an autoregressive rollout planned for reasoning traces

Lark: AI Knowledge Platform for Carey Business School CDHAI · Shipped

AI-powered knowledge platform enabling faculty and staff to query HR policy documents with cited, sourced answers via a RAG pipeline.

  • Built end-to-end: PDF ingestion with Claude Haiku Markdown reformatting, chunked full-text indexing in PostgreSQL, source-cited chat interface, interactive document viewer with passage highlighting
  • Deployed on Azure App Service with React/TypeScript frontend and Bun/Elysia backend, using Azure Blob Storage and LLM tool-calling (GPT + Claude)
  • Shipped 10+ production PRs covering highlight precision, Azure SAS credential fallbacks, deduplication, and PDF text normalization for legacy documents

Multimodal LLM-as-Judge for Academic Paper Quality DSAI · In Progress

Multi-modal pipeline for automated paper quality screening, combining visual analysis and full-text evaluation into a normalized quality score.
