About

I build machine learning and statistical systems that turn uncertain, high-dimensional data into reliable decisions.

My background spans production ML engineering at IBM — anomaly detection, time-series forecasting, LLM-based decision support, and agentic pipelines on terabyte-scale telemetry — and academic research at Johns Hopkins, where I work on longitudinal clinical modeling, probabilistic reasoning pipelines, and LLM evaluation methodology.
