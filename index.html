About

I build machine learning systems that turn uncertain, high-dimensional data into reliable decisions, and software infrastructure that keeps those systems running in production.

Currently a Software Development Engineer intern at AWS on the Transit Gateway team, working on distributed networking infrastructure. I also hold three research appointments at Johns Hopkins: contributing to HopDoc, a clinical reasoning system trained on 8.3M de-identified patient records at CDHAI; developing early prediction models for Severe Maternal Morbidity using Mamba State Space Models at the Bloomberg School of Public Health; and building multimodal LLM-as-judge pipelines for scientific paper evaluation at the Data Science and AI Institute.

Before Hopkins, I spent two years as a Machine Learning Engineer at IBM Systems Lab, shipping anomaly detection, time-series forecasting, ransomware detection, and agentic LLM pipelines into production on terabyte-scale telemetry. That work produced three IBM Redbooks white papers and Gold Author status.

MS in Applied Mathematics at Johns Hopkins (Dean's Master's Fellow, expected May 2027). Six peer-reviewed publications across IEEE, EMNLP, Springer LNNS, and IRE. Open to full-time ML Engineer, AI Engineer, and Software Engineer roles starting May 2027.
